VIP Clothing · SPRD

Innerwear, in the business conversation

17MPR reach across business, retail and trade media
22%average LinkedIn engagement rate

The brief

Innerwear is an everyday category that rarely enters business or cultural conversation. The mandate was to raise visibility for the business, its leadership and the category.

What I did

  • Built a three-pillar strategy: executive positioning and PR, culturally relevant LinkedIn content, and employee advocacy.
  • Created “If Innerwear Was a Therapist”, a humour-led series, and “Employee Myth-Busting”, which put staff in the content.
  • Kept a weekly cadence that balanced creative storytelling with trade insights.

What moved

  • About 15,000 accounts reached, with roughly 100 new professional followers a month.
